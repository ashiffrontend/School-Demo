Admission Process (2026-27)

We make school enrollment transparent, stress-free, and parent-friendly. Follow our 4-step streamlined admission journey.

01

Online Application

Fill out the online admission form with student & guardian details and upload required photograph.

02

Interaction / Assessment

Friendly informal interaction for Kindergarten; basic age-appropriate aptitude evaluation for Class I to VIII.

03

Document Verification

Verification of birth certificate, previous report cards, and residency proof with our admissions desk.

04

Admission Confirmation

Payment of quarterly admission fee, receiving welcome kit, books, uniform guidelines, and class allocation.

Mandatory Documents Checklist

Please ensure you have both original and self-attested photocopies ready for interaction day:

Municipal Birth Certificate (Original + Copy)
Previous School Transfer Certificate (TC) (for Class II to VIII)
Previous Class Report Card / Marksheet
4 Passport-size photographs of the student
2 Passport-size photographs of each Parent/Guardian
Aadhar Card / Residence Proof (Electricity Bill/Passport)
Immunization / Vaccination Record Card

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about admissions, school timings, transport, and curriculum.

As per educational guidelines, for Nursery admission, the child must be 3 years of age as of March 31, 2026. For Class I admission, the child must have completed 6 years of age as of March 31, 2026.
Summer Timings: Nursery to UKG (8:30 AM to 12:30 PM), Class I to VIII (7:45 AM to 1:45 PM).
Winter Timings: Nursery to UKG (9:00 AM to 1:00 PM), Class I to VIII (8:15 AM to 2:15 PM).
We maintain a strict perimeter security with 140+ HD CCTV cameras covering corridors, gates, and play areas. Entry is strictly guarded with visitor ID passes. In buses, live GPS tracking and dedicated female attendants accompany every trip.
The school follows the National Curriculum Framework (NCF) and NEP 2020 guidelines aligned with the CBSE pattern, integrating inquiry-based learning, hands-on STEM education, and holistic co-curricular development.
Yes, our fleet covers over 25 arterial routes across New Delhi and NCR. Parents receive dedicated credentials to track the school bus live via our web portal, along with automated SMS alerts 10 minutes prior to arrival at the bus stop.
